Conflicts Found

Three documents describe three different brands. Worth resolving before the build.

The palette is documented three ways

CLAUDE.md specifies carbon #0E0E0E / brass #C9A961 / bone #F4F1EB. DESIGN.md specifies a dark obsidian #09090B system with an electric cyan #06B6D4 accent. The shipped index.html is neither: a light bone theme with a softer brass #d4a574 and no cyan anywhere. I have treated the code as authoritative.

Inter is banned but shipped

DESIGN.md lists Inter under banned anti-patterns, and the live site loads Inter 300–900 as its only typeface. The cinematic build will use Outfit + JetBrains Mono, which satisfies the ban.

Two palette values fail WCAG as text

Brass #d4a574 on bone scores 1.96:1 and --text-faint #9C978E scores 2.56:1. Both fall below the 3:1 floor for even large text. On the light theme brass works only as a decorative fill — never as a label, link, or button text.

No reachable contact details exist

The source contains no tel: or mailto: link — contact runs entirely through a client-side form with a simulated submission and no backend. The cinematic site needs a real phone and email from you, or its contact section will be decorative too.

Recommended Direction

Invert to dark. This is not a style preference — it is what the measurements require.
